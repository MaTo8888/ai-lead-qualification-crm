Automated Lead Workflow

How a submitted lead becomes a qualified, CRM-tracked sales opportunity

1

Lead Received

Live

A new inquiry arrives via the webhook API or the demo form.

POST /api/leads · FastAPI

2

Validation

Live

Required fields, email format and message content are checked and normalized.

validation.py

3

Duplicate Check

Live

The lead's email and phone are checked against existing CRM records.

duplicate_check.py · SQLite

4

AI Analysis

Live

Produces intent, a short summary and a first-pass qualification.

ai_analysis.py · Anthropic / OpenAI / fallback

5

Qualification

Live

The AI result is combined with deterministic business signals into a final category.

qualification.py

6

CRM Update

Live

The full lead record, including qualification and duplicate status, is stored.

db.py · SQLite

7

Sales Handoff

Live

HOT and WARM leads generate a handoff record in the internal notification queue.

handoff.py · internal queue
